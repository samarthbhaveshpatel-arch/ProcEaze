The problem

Spend visibility usually arrives a month late.

When procurement runs on email and spreadsheets, the numbers only exist after someone compiles them.

  • Month-end surprisesSpend is pieced together from bills and spreadsheets after it has happened.
  • No view of complianceLeadership can't see how much is bought on contract and how much isn't.
  • Hidden bottlenecksSlow approvals and stuck orders don't show up until someone complains.

The ProcEaze way

Procurement reporting and analytics for every role.

Every requisition, approval and PO runs through ProcEaze, so spend dashboards and reports are always current.

Company-wide spend

Spend by plant, department, category and vendor.

Role-specific views
