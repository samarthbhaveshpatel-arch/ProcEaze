Client onboarding

We set you up. Your KAM stays with you.

For enterprises onboarded by the ProcEaze team, these are the four steps to go live.

  1. 01

    Client credentials issued

    Your workspace is created and credentials are issued to your administrators.

  2. 02

    Catalogue & ARC configured

    Your catalogue, contracted ARC prices, MOQs and validity are loaded.

  3. 03

    Org hierarchy built

    Departments, users, roles and approval chains of one to five levels are set up.

  4. 04

    Dedicated KAM assigned

    A named Key Account Manager becomes your single point of contact.

Managed procurement & sourcing

Three routes for off-contract needs.

Whichever route you take, the result goes through NFA approval and becomes a purchase order.

Through RFQ

Raise an RFQ to your vendors, compare quotes side by side and choose the best offer.

Through reverse auction

Run a live auction with L1/L2/L3 ranking, reserve price and anti-sniping.

ProcEaze as vendor

Choose ProcEaze as the vendor. The requisition goes straight to purchase approval and ProcEaze handles sourcing, pricing and fulfilment.
