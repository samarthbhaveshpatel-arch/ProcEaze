About ProcEaze

Built by people who run supply chains.

Contact Us Explore the platform

Our story

From an internal tool to a procurement platform.

We didn't start with software. We started with procurement: thousands of industrial items, many sites, contracted prices that didn't always reach the order, and approvals that lived in inboxes.

So we built the tool we needed. Because it was built by people who run supply chains, it follows how industrial procurement actually works in India: annual rate contracts, layered approvals, NFAs and a long tail of MRO categories.

  1. 01
    Origin

    Built for our own operations

    ProcEaze started as an in-house tool for procurement across our own supply-chain operations in Ahmedabad.

  2. 02
    RProc

    The first version

    The first version, RProc, ran our own procurement: catalogue buying, approvals and purchase orders, replacing email and spreadsheets.

  3. 03
    ProcEaze

    A platform for every enterprise

    RProc evolved into ProcEaze: a multi-client platform with complete data isolation, configurable approval hierarchies and a dedicated Key Account Manager for every client.

  4. 04
    Today

    Sourcing, auctions and AI

    The Sourcing Desk, Reverse Auction Portal and Vendor Portal bring sourcing onto the platform. The AI Price Negotiator is in early access.
