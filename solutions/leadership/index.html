Challenges

Procurement control shouldn't depend on month-end reports.

What leadership typically faces before ProcEaze.

  • Spend seen too lateNumbers arrive after month-end, compiled by hand.
  • Contract leakageNegotiated ARC rates don't always reach the order.
  • Policy depends on peopleThe Delegation of Authority lives in a document, not in the process.
  • No single source of truthEach plant and category runs on its own tools.
