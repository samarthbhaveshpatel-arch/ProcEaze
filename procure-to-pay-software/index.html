The basics

What is procure-to-pay (P2P) software?

Procure-to-pay, or P2P, is the chain of steps from the moment someone needs something to the moment the vendor is paid: requisition, approval, purchase order, delivery, invoice and payment. P2P software keeps those steps connected, so each one starts from the last instead of from a fresh email.

When the chain is broken, the same order is typed three times, approvals can't be traced to the PO, and nobody can say whether the price paid matched the price agreed.

ProcEaze keeps requisition, approval, purchase order and delivery on one record today, at your contracted price. Payment and reconciliation are on our roadmap, and we label them that way.

Where the P2P chain breaks

  • Retyped at every stepThe request, the approval and the PO live in different places.
  • Untraceable approvalsNobody can link a PO back to who approved it.
  • Price driftThe rate on the PO isn't the rate in the contract.

Purchase requisition management

Purchase requisition management: from cart to approved PR.

Purchase requisition software should make the right request the easy one. In ProcEaze, a buyer builds a cart from the contracted catalogue, the cart is checked against contract price, MOQ and validity, and one click raises the purchase requisition (PR). The approval route is shown before it is submitted.

  • PR raised in one click from a validated cart
  • ARC price, MOQ and validity checked per line

Purchase order management

Purchase order management: POs on contracted terms, tracked to delivery.

PO management in ProcEaze starts where approval ends. Once a requisition or NFA is fully approved, the purchase order is issued to the vendor at the contracted rate, with payment and delivery terms visible on the PO. The order is then tracked through confirmation and delivery, and the PO is stored securely with its approvals.

  • PO issued to the vendor after approval
  • Catalogue orders priced at ARC rates
  • Payment and delivery terms visible on each PO
  • Tracked through confirmation and delivery, with a downloadable audit trail
