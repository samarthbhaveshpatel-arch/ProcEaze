The basics

What is procurement automation?

Procurement automation is software doing the repeatable parts of buying, so people don't have to. Most of the effort in indirect and MRO procurement goes into checking a price, finding the right approver, chasing a sign-off and retyping an order.

None of those steps needs judgement. They need a rule, applied the same way every time. Procurement automation software applies the rule, and leaves the judgement to the people accountable for it.

ProcEaze automates the path from cart to purchase order. Contract prices are applied for you, carts are checked before they are submitted, requests route themselves through your Delegation of Authority (DoA), and every action is written to the audit trail.

The manual work automation removes

  • Checking prices by handEvery order needs someone to confirm the contracted rate.
  • Chasing approvalsRequesters follow up by phone because nobody knows where a request sits.
  • Rebuilding the recordWho approved what has to be pieced together later from email.

Approval workflows

Approval workflows that route themselves by your Delegation of Authority.

Approval workflow automation is the heart of procurement workflow automation. ProcEaze configures an approval chain for each client, from one level to five, to mirror its Delegation of Authority. Requisitions and NFAs move through it on their own, and every approver sees what is waiting in a role-specific queue.

  • Chains of one to five levels, configured per client
  • Approve, reject or send back in one click, with comments
  • NFAs route through the same hierarchy as requisitions
  • Every decision time-stamped and attributed
