FAQ

Procurement services: common questions

Procurement services cover the work of buying for an organisation: finding and pricing items, raising and approving requisitions, issuing orders and getting goods delivered. ProcEaze offers this two ways: as a platform your own team runs, or as Managed Procurement, where ProcEaze supplies for you.

With the Platform (SaaS), your teams run procurement on ProcEaze with your own vendors and their ARC prices. With Managed Procurement, you choose ProcEaze as the vendor on a requisition and ProcEaze handles sourcing, pricing and fulfilment. Both run on the same platform, with the same approvals, dashboards and audit trail.

Companies wanting to outsource indirect / MRO buying. Enterprises with an in-house procurement team usually start with the Platform.
