What we cover

B2B procurement services for indirect and MRO spend.

Indirect and MRO buying is high in volume and low in visibility. Most of the effort goes into finding items, prices and approvers, not into the purchase itself.

ProcEaze brings that buying into one place: 5,00,000+ products across 56+ categories, contracted prices on the product, cart validation, one-click requisitions and approvals that route themselves. The categories below form the core of a typical catalogue.

Corporate procurement

Corporate procurement services for every department.

Corporate purchasing spans offices, plants, sites and departments, each with its own requesters and approvers. ProcEaze gives them one catalogue and one set of rules, and gives leadership one view of the spend.

One catalogue for every site

Plants, offices and project sites order from the same catalogue, configured for your company.

Approvals to your DoA

Chains of one to five levels, built around your Delegation of Authority.

Departments and roles

Users, departments and roles are set up during onboarding, so each team sees its own queue.

NFAs and POs

Notes for Approval and purchase orders run through the same hierarchy.

Spend by department

Role-based dashboards show spend by plant, department, category and vendor.

Clean records for finance

Every order, approval and rejection is time-stamped and downloadable.

B2B and corporate procurement services: FAQs

B2B procurement services help one business buy what it needs from others: finding items, getting the right price, routing approvals, placing orders and getting goods delivered. ProcEaze provides this through its platform, or through Managed Procurement, where ProcEaze supplies for you.

Corporate procurement services cover company-wide buying of indirect goods for offices, plants and sites. With ProcEaze, every department orders from one catalogue, approvals follow your Delegation of Authority, and leadership sees spend on live dashboards.

The ProcEaze platform covers 5,00,000+ products across 56+ categories, including safety & PPE, MRO, electricals, fasteners, bearings, stationery, office supplies, IT, hand tools and industrial machinery. Your catalogue is configured for your company by your Key Account Manager.
