The problem

Why annual rate contract procurement breaks down.

Procurement negotiates the rate. Then it has to survive every buyer, every site and every order for a year.

  • Rates live in spreadsheetsBuyers cannot see the ARC, so orders go out at whatever price is quoted.
  • Expiry goes unnoticedContracts lapse quietly and orders continue at old or unagreed prices.
  • Compliance is hard to proveFinance cannot show that orders matched the contract without manual checks.

The ARC lifecycle

Annual rate contract procurement services, from contract to renewal.

You agree ARC terms with your vendors. From there, ProcEaze and your Key Account Manager carry the contract through every order until it is renewed.

  1. Contract agreed

    ARC terms finalised with your vendors

  2. KAM loads it

    Prices, MOQs and validity configured

  3. Buyer orders

    ARC price applied automatically

  4. Approval

    Routes through your hierarchy

  5. PO at ARC

    Issued at the contracted rate

  6. Renewal

    Tracked and handled by your KAM

FAQ

ARC procurement services: FAQs

An annual rate contract is an agreement with a vendor that fixes prices for a set of items for a period, usually a year. Buyers then order against those rates as needs arise, instead of asking for a fresh quote each time.

ARC procurement services cover the work of running rate contracts day to day: loading contract prices, MOQs and validity into the catalogue, making sure orders use those prices, and tracking renewals. At ProcEaze, your Key Account Manager does this, and the platform locks the ARC price at the point of order.
