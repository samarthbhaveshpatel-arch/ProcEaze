The problem

Indirect buying has too many hand-offs.

A single requisition for safety shoes or bearings can pass through emails, phone calls, quotes and follow-ups before anything arrives. Nobody owns it end to end, and nothing is on one record.

  • Too many vendors for small ordersSeparate suppliers for PPE, electricals, stationery and spares, each with its own process.
  • Prices chased on every orderBuyers ask, wait and compare before they can place a routine order.
  • No trail from request to deliveryRequests by email or WhatsApp leave no record of who asked, who approved and what arrived.

The process

The end-to-end procurement process, step by step.

Managed Procurement runs inside the ProcEaze platform, so every stage uses the same approvals, dashboards and audit trail as the rest of your buying.

  1. Requisition

    Raised from the catalogue, with ProcEaze as the vendor

  2. Approval

    Purchase approval in your 1–5 level hierarchy

  3. Sourcing & pricing

    Handled by ProcEaze

  4. Fulfilment

    Supplied by ProcEaze

  5. Delivery

    Order tracked through to delivery

  6. Audit trail

    Every step time-stamped and attributed
