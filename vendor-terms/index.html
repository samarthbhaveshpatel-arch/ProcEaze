Draft structure for legal review. This page sets out the intended structure only. Final wording must be reviewed and approved by legal counsel before publication.

1. Eligibility

Who may register as a vendor, including GST registration requirements.

2. Registration and verification

Information and documents required for verification: GSTIN, PAN, company registration, bank details, product catalogue / price list and authorisation letters where applicable. Explain the right to accept or decline registrations.

3. Accuracy of information

Vendors must keep their details, documents and price lists accurate and current.

4. RFQs and quotations

Rules for responding to RFQs, validity of quotes and confidentiality of buyer requirements.

5. Reverse auctions

Auction rules, including bid validity, minimum decrements, anti-sniping extensions and prohibited conduct such as collusion. To be finalised before the Auction Portal launches.

6. AI-assisted negotiation

Disclosure that vendors may negotiate with an AI agent acting for a buyer, that transcripts are recorded, and that final awards are made by the buyer.

7. Purchase orders and fulfilment

How POs are issued, accepted and fulfilled, and how delivery is confirmed.

8. Payment terms

Payment terms are set out in each purchase order. Details to be confirmed.

9. Confidentiality

Obligations regarding buyer information, prices and documents.

10. Suspension and termination

Grounds and process for suspending or removing a vendor.

11. Contact

Vendor support and legal contact details to be confirmed.

Last updated: to be confirmed on publication.
