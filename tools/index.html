Why free procurement templates?

Much of indirect and MRO procurement still runs on documents that each buyer writes from scratch: an RFQ in an email, a comparison in a spreadsheet, a rate contract copied from last year. Starting from a consistent template means every supplier gets the same information, every comparison uses the same criteria, and nothing important is left out.

These templates are written for Indian procurement teams: rupees and lakh, GST and HSN codes, annual rate contracts, notes for approval (NFAs) and multi-site delivery.

How they fit together

Use the savings calculator to size the opportunity, the RFQ generator to collect quotes, the supplier evaluation template to compare them, and the annual rate contract template to lock in the result. The MRO checklist covers the whole cycle so you can spot gaps.

New to a term? The procurement glossary explains ARC, MOQ, NFA, L1 and more, and the guide to annual rate contract procurement goes deeper on rate contracts.
