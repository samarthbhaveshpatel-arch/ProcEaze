General

ProcEaze is a B2B procurement intelligence and e-procurement platform for industrial, MRO and indirect procurement. It brings catalogue buying, approvals, sourcing, reverse auctions, AI price negotiation and purchase orders into one workspace.

Indian enterprises with industrial, MRO and indirect spend, including manufacturing and process plants, automotive and auto components, infrastructure and construction, energy and renewables, logistics, ports and ICDs, and facility management and corporate offices. Inside a company, it serves leadership, procurement, department heads, buyers, finance and vendors.

ProcEaze was built by a team that runs industrial supply-chain operations in Ahmedabad, India, and started as an in-house procurement tool before becoming a platform for every enterprise.

ProcEaze Platform (SaaS), where your teams run procurement on ProcEaze with your own vendors, and ProcEaze Managed Procurement, where you choose ProcEaze as the vendor for a requisition and ProcEaze handles sourcing, pricing and fulfilment.

Pricing is on request and depends on your engagement model and scope. Contact us and our team will discuss it with you.

Every client gets a named Key Account Manager who handles configuration, renewals, catalogue updates, escalations and periodic spend reports.

Catalogue

5,00,000+ products across 56+ categories, including safety & PPE, MRO, electricals, fasteners, bearings & power transmission, paper & stationery, office supplies, IT & electronics, hand tools, and industrial plants & machinery.

Your dedicated Key Account Manager configures your catalogue and ARC prices, and handles renewals and catalogue updates.

Yes. With Managed Procurement, choose ProcEaze as the vendor on a requisition. It goes straight to purchase approval in your hierarchy.

ARC Pricing

ARC stands for annual rate contract. ProcEaze loads your contracted prices into the catalogue and locks them at the point of order, so 100% of catalogue requisitions are priced at ARC rates.

No. ARC prices are applied automatically and cannot be edited at order. Items outside the contract go through RFQ, reverse auction or AI negotiation instead.

Your Key Account Manager handles renewals and price updates.

Approvals

Each client gets its own approval chain, configured from one level to five to match your Delegation of Authority.

Approve, reject or send back a request in one click. Every action is time-stamped and attributed in a permanent audit trail.

Yes. ProcEaze provides role-specific dashboards for directors, department heads, managers, buyers and finance.

Yes. Email notifications are live, so approvers know when a request reaches them.

Sourcing

Off-contract needs go through RFQ, reverse auction or AI negotiation, then NFA approval and purchase order.

The Sourcing Desk brings RFQs, auctions, vendor selection, NFAs and POs into one desk for procurement teams.

A Note for Approval. In ProcEaze, it carries the quotes, ranking and recommendation through your approval hierarchy before a purchase order is issued.

Auctions

Yes. The Reverse Auction Portal runs live reverse auctions, and you can try a simulation on the Reverse Auctions page.

English reverse auctions and Japanese descending-clock auctions, with ceiling price, minimum decrement, a timer, L1 / L2 / L3 live ranking, reserve price, anti-sniping and bid history.

AI Negotiator

It is an AI agent that negotiates with vendors on your behalf, over chat or voice, countering quotes against your target price and bringing back a ranked shortlist with full transcripts.

No. The buyer remains in control. The AI negotiates and recommends, exceptions are escalated to the buyer, buyers can take over at any time, and awards go through your NFA approval.

It is in early access for selected clients. You can ask to join through the demo request form.

Vendors

Register once on the Become a Vendor page. During verification you'll be asked for GSTIN, PAN, company registration, bank details, a product catalogue or price list, and authorisation letters where applicable.

The Vendor Portal is where vendors receive RFQs matched to their categories, take part in auctions and track awarded purchase orders.

Security

Each client's data is completely isolated. Users sign in with passwordless OTP, access is role-based, sessions use encrypted tokens, every action is recorded in a permanent audit trail, and documents are stored securely in the cloud.

SSO is on the roadmap. Today, users sign in with passwordless OTP.
