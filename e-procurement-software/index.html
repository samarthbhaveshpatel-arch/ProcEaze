The basics

What is e-procurement software?

E-procurement software is the online version of how a company buys. People find items in a digital catalogue, see the agreed price, raise a request and get it approved, all in one online procurement system instead of across phones, inboxes and spreadsheets.

In many plants and offices, buying still happens offline. A request comes in on WhatsApp, someone checks a rate in Excel, a vendor is called for a quote and the approval arrives by email. It works, but nobody can see the whole picture, and the contracted price rarely survives the trip.

ProcEaze replaces that with one e-procurement platform. Your contracted catalogue, requisitions, approvals and purchase orders sit in one place, with one login, and every step is on the record.

What offline buying costs you

  • Requests on WhatsAppNo trail of who asked for what, or who said yes.
  • Rates in a spreadsheetBuyers can't see the ARC, so orders go out at whatever is quoted.
  • Quotes in inboxesEvery RFQ ends in a comparison sheet built by hand.

Sourcing Desk

RFQ / RFx management

RFQ and RFx management: e-sourcing for everything off-contract.

RFx is the family of sourcing requests: RFI, RFP and RFQ. In ProcEaze, e-sourcing is built around the RFQ. When an item isn't on contract, it goes to RFQ instead of an ad-hoc purchase. The Sourcing Desk brings RFQ creation, quote comparison, vendor selection, the Note for Approval (NFA) and the purchase order into one place.

  • Raise an RFQ from a requirement and invite vendors
  • Compare quotes side by side with L1 / L2 / L3 ranking
  • Raise an NFA with quotes and recommendation attached
  • Approved NFAs become purchase orders
