Industries · Automotive & Auto Components

Automotive procurement for plants that run to the minute.

Automotive OEM and auto component plants buy a long tail of MRO spares, tooling, fasteners and safety supplies to keep production lines on schedule. ProcEaze puts them in one catalogue at ARC prices, with plant-wise approvals and live spend visibility.

Challenges

Indirect buying can't hold up the line.

When a spare or consumable is missing, production waits.

  • Line-critical sparesBearings, belts and maintenance spares are needed fast, at the agreed price.
  • Many plants and shiftsEach plant and shift raises its own requirements and approvals.
  • Price drift on repeat itemsThe same consumables are bought again and again, often at different rates.
  • Shop-floor safetyPPE has to be the right specification for every line and every shift.

How ProcEaze helps

Plant-floor procurement, standardised.

One catalogue

MRO spares, bearings, fasteners, hand tools, PPE and electricals for every plant, in one place.

Catalogue & Buying

ARC prices everywhere

The same contracted price at every site, locked at the point of order.
