      The problem

      Approvals stall when they live in inboxes.

      Without a fixed route, requests wait, requesters chase, and nobody can say later who signed off.

      • No fixed routeNobody is sure who should approve what, or in which order.
      • No visibilityRequests sit unseen, and requesters chase approvers by phone.
      • No recordSign-offs on email are hard to find and harder to audit.

      The ProcEaze way

      Your Delegation of Authority, built into every request.

      ProcEaze configures an approval hierarchy for each client. Requests move through it automatically, with role-specific dashboards at every level.

      1–5 approval levels

      Every client gets its own chain, configured to its Delegation of Authority.

      Approve, reject or send back

      Approvers act in one click and can add comments for the requester.

      Role-specific dashboards

      Directors, heads, managers and buyers each see their own queue and spend.

      NFA approvals

      Notes for Approval route through the same hierarchy with quotes and ranking attached.

      Email notifications

      Approvers are notified when a request reaches them.

      Permanent audit trail

      Every approval, rejection and send-back is time-stamped and attributed.

      Inside the product

      Every approver, one clear queue.

      Approvers see the request, the price basis and where it sits in the chain, then act in one click. Send-backs return to the requester with comments.

      • Director → Head → Manager → Buyer, or any chain you need
      • Pending time visible for every request
      • Decisions recorded with name and time
