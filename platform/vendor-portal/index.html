The problem

Suppliers deserve a clear, fair process too.

For vendors, enterprise procurement often means phone calls, scattered emails and little idea where a quote stands.

  • RFQs by phone and emailRequirements arrive in every format, through every channel.
  • Opaque outcomesVendors rarely know how their quote ranked or why.
  • PO follow-upsTracking awarded orders means chasing buyers for updates.

The ProcEaze way

One portal for every opportunity.

Register once, get verified, and see RFQs, auctions and POs from ProcEaze clients in one place.

Matched RFQs

RFQs routed to you based on the categories you supply.

Quote submission

Submit quotes in a consistent format, on time.

Auction participation

Bid in live reverse auctions with transparent ranking.

PO tracking

Follow awarded purchase orders through to delivery.

Profile & documents

GSTIN, PAN, registration and bank details kept on file.

Workflow

From registration to delivery.

  1. Register

    Company and contact details

  2. Verification

    GSTIN, PAN and documents

  3. Matched RFQs

    Based on your categories

  4. Quote or bid

    RFQ response or live auction

  5. PO awarded

    Tracked in the portal

  6. Deliver

    Delivery confirmed on record
