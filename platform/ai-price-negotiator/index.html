The problem

Negotiation is where buyers' time goes.

Every quote invites a counter, and every counter is another call, email or WhatsApp thread.

  • Endless back-and-forthBuyers spend hours per requirement chasing revised quotes.
  • Inconsistent outcomesResults depend on who negotiates and how much time they have.
  • No record of the conversationPhone negotiations leave nothing to show approvers or auditors.

The ProcEaze way

Consistent negotiation, at the scale of your requirements.

The AI agent applies the same discipline to every vendor and every requirement, and documents all of it.

Vendor discovery

Find vendors for the requirement from your vendor pool.

GSTIN verification

Check vendor GSTINs before negotiation begins.

Target price & benchmark

Negotiate against your target, with a benchmark for context.

Voice & chat negotiation

Negotiate with vendors over chat or voice.

Negotiation transcript

Every exchange recorded and attached to the requirement.

Reliability score

Each vendor's offer shown with a reliability score.

Live leaderboard

Offers ranked in real time as negotiations progress.

Escalation

Anything outside your policy goes to the buyer.

Buyer override

Take over any negotiation at any time. The AI pauses.

Human in the loop

Buyer remains in control.

The AI Price Negotiator is an assistant, not a decision-maker. It works within the targets you set, escalates what falls outside them and stops the moment a buyer takes over.

  • Negotiates only within the target and policy you set
  • Escalates exceptions, such as credit terms, to the buyer
  • The buyer can take over any negotiation at any time
  • A full transcript is kept for every conversation
  • The final award goes through your NFA approval
