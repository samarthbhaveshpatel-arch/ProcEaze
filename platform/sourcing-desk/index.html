The problem

Sourcing runs across too many tools.

Every off-contract requirement turns into a chain of emails, attachments and spreadsheets.

  • Quotes in inboxesRFQs go out by email and quotes come back in every format.
  • Manual comparisonsTeams rebuild the same comparison sheet for every requirement.
  • Gaps between stepsVendor selection, NFA and PO are disconnected, so context gets lost.

The ProcEaze way

Everything off-contract, handled in one flow.

Raise the RFQ, compare quotes, run an auction or AI negotiation, then move straight to NFA and PO without leaving the desk.

RFQ creation

Raise an RFQ from a requirement and invite vendors.

Quote comparison

Send to auction
