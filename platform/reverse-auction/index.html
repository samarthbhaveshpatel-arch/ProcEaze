English reverse auction

Bidders see the current L1 price and their own rank, and bid down in minimum decrements until the clock runs out. A bid in the final seconds extends the clock, so nobody wins by sniping.

Japanese descending clock

The price starts at the ceiling and drops by a fixed decrement every round. Bidders confirm to stay in. The last bidder standing wins at the final price.

The problem

Price discovery by email is slow and opaque.

Sequential quote rounds take days and give vendors little reason to sharpen their price.

  • Round after roundRevised quotes trickle in over days of follow-up.
  • No competitive tensionVendors can't see where they stand, so they hold their price.
  • Hard to defendThe final choice is difficult to justify without a clear record of bids.

The ProcEaze way

Live, transparent, and on the record.

Invite qualified vendors, set the ceiling, reserve and decrement, and let the market find the price.

Ceiling price

Set the opening price that bids must beat.

Minimum decrement

Each new bid must improve on L1 by at least the decrement.

Timer & anti-sniping

Bids in the final seconds extend the clock automatically.

L1 / L2 / L3 live ranking

Vendors see their rank, never each other's names.

Reserve price

A buyer-only reserve shows whether the result is acceptable.

Bid history

Every bid time-stamped, for the NFA and audit.

Workflow

From requirement to purchase order.

  1. Requirement

    Lot, quantity and specification

  2. Invite vendors

    Qualified vendors only

  3. Live auction

    English or Japanese format

  4. Ranking

    L1 / L2 / L3 at close

  5. NFA approval

    Your approvers decide

  6. Purchase order

    Issued to the awarded vendor
