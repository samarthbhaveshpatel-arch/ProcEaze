Six layers of control

How ProcEaze protects procurement data.

Complete data isolation per client

Each client's users, catalogue, ARC prices, vendors, documents and records are kept separate from every other client.

  • A separate workspace for each client
  • No shared pricing or catalogue data between clients
  • Client-specific hierarchy and roles

Passwordless OTP sign-in

Users sign in with a one-time password. There are no passwords to steal, reuse or reset.

  • One-time password at sign-in
  • No passwords to leak or reuse
  • Accounts tied to the user's email

Role-based access

What each user can see and do is defined by their role in your organisation.

  • Buyers see their orders
  • Approvers see their queue
  • Leadership and finance views by role

Permanent audit trail

Every order, approval and rejection is time-stamped, attributed and downloadable.

  • Who did what, and when
  • Approvals, rejections and send-backs
  • Export whenever audit asks

Secure cloud document storage

Quotes, NFAs and purchase orders are stored securely in the cloud, linked to the transaction they belong to.

  • Documents linked to each transaction
  • No attachments lost in inboxes
  • Access follows role permissions

Encrypted tokens

Sessions are secured with encrypted tokens, tied to the signed-in user and their role.

  • Encrypted session tokens
  • Access tied to user and role
  • Signed-in state verified by the platform
